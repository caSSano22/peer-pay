Merchant FAQ

Everything you need to know about accepting Peer Pay checkouts.

Does Peer Pay hold my money?

+
No. Peer Pay is completely non-custodial. Funds move directly from your customer to your self-custody wallet or linked bank account. Peer never touches or holds merchant capital.

How long does payment verification take?

+
Most payments verify in under 15 seconds. Once verified, webhooks automatically update your order status to PAID in your e-commerce store.

What payment apps are supported?

+
Customers can pay using Venmo, Cash App, Revolut, Wise, Zelle, PayPal, Chime, Monzo, and native Robinhood Wallet USD-R / USDC stablecoins.
